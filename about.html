Who we are

IDisplay Inc. is a premier full-service provider in the exhibition, display, and events industry in the Philippines, proudly serving clients since 2013. With over a decade of experience, we have built a reputation for delivering innovative, functional, and visually compelling solutions that help brands stand out and connect meaningfully with their audience.

We offer end-to-end services, from conceptual design and planning to fabrication, installation, and post-event support, ensuring a seamless and efficient process tailored to each client’s needs and objectives.

What we specialize in

Our team specializes in custom-built exhibit booths, commercial interiors, retail displays, roadshows, and promotional installations, combining creativity with durability and cost-efficiency.

With in-house design and production capabilities, we maintain full quality control while remaining flexible and responsive.

  • Custom-built exhibit booths
  • Commercial interiors
  • Retail displays
  • Roadshows
  • Promotional installations
